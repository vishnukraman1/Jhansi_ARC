# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **High-End Private Clients & Institutional Patrons**: Individuals, family trusts, and cultural foundations seeking bespoke, site-sensitive residential or commercial architecture with uncompromising environmental and structural integrity.
2. **Architectural Juries & Curators**: Design committees evaluating design rigor, structural innovations (mass-timber, rammed earth, board-formed concrete), and technical drawings.

## Product Purpose

A digital monograph and interactive architectural studio showcasing the individual works of Vishnu Kraman (V. Kraman Studio). It proves design excellence through two synchronized lenses: visceral atmospheric photography and precise interactive technical evidence (2D vector CAD drawings and 3D massing studies).

## Positioning

Unlike generic creative portfolios that rely solely on aesthetic photography, this studio showcases complete structural honesty—pairing every project with full spatial narratives (Problem, Process, Solution), comprehensive specification matrices, and interactive drafting blueprints that let visitors inspect room zones, dimension strings, and structural assemblies.

## Operating Context

Visitors view the portfolio on desktop workstations and mobile devices. Key interactive capabilities include zooming and panning vector floor plans, toggling drafting layers (Grid, Dimensions, Annotations), switching between dark drafting mode and light print sheets, exploring 3D exploded massing models, and submitting architectural commission briefs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single Page App Architecture**: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, Three.js, Lucide Icons, and DXF Parser.
- **2D Vector Drafting Engine**: Interactive SVG/DXF viewer with pan/zoom, room hover HUDs, dimension inspect, and layer controls.
- **3D Spatial Massing Studies**: Procedural Three.js models with exploded assembly sliders and material shaders.
- **Commission Intake**: Direct multi-field intake specifying project typology, site location, timeline, and scope.

## Brand Commitments

- **Aesthetic**: Alpine Swiss precision, minimalist restraint, sculptural silence.
- **Palette**: Gallery Off-White (`#F7F7F5`), Obsidian Soot (`#121212`), Framing Grey (`#E0E0DE`), Blueprint Accents (`#3B82F6`).
- **Typography**: Inter (Body & Display) and JetBrains Mono (Technical Coordinates, Matrices & Labels).

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete project narratives and specification matrices for 6 flagship projects across Switzerland, Norway, Denmark, USA, and Japan.
- Embedded vector SVG technical plans (Floor Plans, Sections, Site Plans, Axonometrics) and AutoCAD DXF file parsing.
- Verified credentials: ETH Zürich (M.Arch Summa Cum Laude), Snohetta / Kengo Kuma / Olson Kundig practice history, European Architecture Emerging Voice awards.

## Product Principles

1. **Structural Honesty Over Decoration**: Every visual element serves an architectural purpose; interfaces recede to let structural form and technical drafting speak.
2. **Visceral Experience Meets Technical Rigor**: Pair high-impact photography with verifiable engineering data, material life-cycle metrics, and dimensioned vector drawings.
3. **Silent Spatial Rhythm**: Generous whitespace, disciplined alignment, and subtle micro-motion that feels like natural daylight shifts rather than digital noise.
